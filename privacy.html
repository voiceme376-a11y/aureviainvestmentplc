Privacy Notice

This is a product template and must be reviewed and finalized for the operator's jurisdiction, provider agreements and applicable privacy requirements before production use.

Information collected

The platform can store account credentials, contact details, profile information, verification state, payment references, bank-beneficiary metadata and security/audit events needed to operate the account.

Financial information

Payment credentials should be handled by the configured payment provider. The platform should not store raw card details or provider secret keys in the browser.

Access and retention

Authorized operations personnel can access server-side account records according to role permissions. Retention periods should be configured to meet applicable requirements.
